Quiet

Instagram, minus the parts that keep you there.

Privacy Support Source

Your feed, your stories, your messages, your profile — loaded the way you know them. Reels, Explore and the accounts suggested between your friends are not there. You sign in on Instagram’s own page, so your password never touches Quiet.

You choose how many minutes a day you want. When they are gone the app closes for the day. You can lower that number whenever you like, and it takes effect at once; you can raise it once a week, and it starts the next day — never in the moment you want five more minutes.

What it does not do

  • No account, no server of its own, no analytics, and nothing sent anywhere the developer can reach.
  • No streaks, no weekly report, nothing to check. One optional reminder a day, at an hour you choose, and it stays quiet on a day you have already opened Instagram.
  • No countdown on screen. What time is left is in the panel, for the moments you actually want to know.

Quiet is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.
