Quiet

Instagram, minus the parts that keep you there.

Quiet shows Instagram's mobile site with Reels, Explore and suggested accounts removed, and a daily time limit that can only be raised once a week — and never in the moment you want five more minutes.

What it is

  • Your feed, your stories, your messages, your profile.
  • No Reels. No Explore. No accounts suggested between your friends.
  • A limit you choose. Lowering it is immediate and free; raising it takes effect the next day, once a week at most.
  • No account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising.

What it is not

Quiet is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta. It is a browser that shows one site and refuses a few of its addresses. You sign in on Instagram's own page; your password never touches Quiet.
