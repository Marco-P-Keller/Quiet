How it works, and what to do when it does something you did not expect.

Quiet Privacy

Where are the settings?

Touch and hold the top edge of the screen — the strip where the clock and the battery are. The panel has your time today, your daily limit, and a field for finding someone. Quiet says this once when you set it up, and again on the first launch of each of the next two days.

Using VoiceOver, the same strip is an element called Quiet settings; activate it in the usual way.

Why can I not raise my limit?

You can lower it whenever you like, and it takes effect immediately. You can raise it once a week, and the new number starts the next day. The screen tells you which day you may ask again.

This is the whole point of the app rather than an oversight. The moment you want five more minutes is the moment least able to judge whether you should have them.

When does the day start?

At 4 a.m., not at midnight, so that a late evening belongs to the evening it feels like rather than to the next morning. Changing your phone’s time zone does not hand out a new day: the day you are in keeps the ending it was given when it began, and the next one starts at 4 a.m. wherever you have landed.

Where is search?

Search is the doorway to Explore, so Quiet does not have it. To reach a particular person, type their name into Find someone in the panel and it goes straight to that profile.

Instagram asked me to open its app

Quiet declines that one link, and says so: Quiet is your Instagram here. It is the only link you can deliberately tap that the app refuses, and it is refused because following it would undo everything the app is for in a single tap.

Something looks wrong on the page

Quiet loads Instagram’s real mobile website and hides the parts it removes. When Instagram changes its pages, something Quiet hides may come back or something it keeps may go missing. That is a bug worth reporting — say which screen and what you saw.

A red banner appeared

Quiet has exactly one red banner and it means one of two things: files are missing from the app, or your phone’s keychain is refusing to save. Either way the limit is not being kept. Delete the app and install it again; if the banner comes back, report it.

Will deleting the app reset my limit?

No, and that is deliberate. The limit is kept outside the app so that reinstalling is not a way around it. Erasing the phone, or restoring it as a new device, does start over.

Getting in touch

Open an issue at github.com/Marco-P-Keller/Quiet/issues. Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
